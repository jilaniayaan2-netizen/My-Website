Sol Orbis
Sol Orbis are the dominant species of plant in the nothern hemisphere of the western continent. This is due to it both its massive size(40m tall) and due to it having incredibly resiliant, thick skin. This same armored skin also covers the large sun collecting disk on the top of its body. These giants mainly live in the northen grasslands and less in the jungle, this is because the rainforests down south are too congested for these giants to grow. Sol Orbis provides a food source for the Sol Comedenti(check the Fauna Page), the largest land animals of C-100.
Cyathos Paludis
These plants fill a similar ecological niche as mangroves, acting as shelter for land animals living in the swamp from the tides and protects the smaller animals from predators. Cyathos Paludis have also evolved very dense but flexiable wood that makes them able to survive the harsh tides of the Sea of Dragons(aka The Eastern Interior Seaway). The cup-like structures that grow all over it's body are reproductive organs of the plants, similar to flowers on earth.
Victor Terram
While the Sol Comedenti(check the Fauna Page) are the largest land animals of C-100, the Victor Terram are the largest living organism. In the midwest of the western continent, they dominant an astonishing area of 5000km. While they all look like seperate organisms, they are all linked together via a network similar to mycelium networks in mushrooms and all share information and nutrients. This makes the millions, if not billions of indiviuals one super organism. They are the sole reason for the Poncu Savvannah due to preventing any other plants from growing there, effectively acting as a barrier from the rainforest.
Folium Caeli
These plants opted out of the intense competition that takes place between plants on the ground by instead evolving helium sacks to float in the air. Due to the planet's low gravity and thick atmosphere, they are able to remain suspended in the air for their entire lives, carried around by air currents. These plants act as resting places for flying creatures and as places for Volitare Silvas(Check the Fauna Page) to lay their eggs.
Colligator Aauae
Colligator Aauae are a highly specialized plants that live in the Smokey Forest biome deep in the caverns of C-100. The Smokey Forest is a biome created by a creature called Dator Vitae(Check the Fauna Page), in this biome there is no light, so how does a plant survive without light? These plants instead collect rainwater and condensed water from the cave ceiling which they then convert into sugars, similar to photosythesis but it takes out the need for sunlight entirely.
Fructus Deorum
Fructus Deorum are very typical fruit bearing plants that produce purple berries, there purple berries have a very different make up than most fruits on earth. Elira(not Miku, name change I did for a rewrite) uses these berries to make her(I accidently wrote him on the page) Purple Berry Brew which is similar to a latte or strong coffee.

Volitare Silva
Evolutionary History
Volitare Silva evolved very uniquely due to being one of the few "flying" aliens that has 4 limbs instead of the standard six limbs that most flying creatures have. These creatures' evolutionary history is very similar to that of whales on Earth. Instead of going starting out on land, Volitare Silva started out in the water. Since the atmosphere of C-100 is denser, it was much easier for them to slowly(very slowly) evolve to go into the air. Their swimbladders, which they used to control their bouyancy in the water then evolved to instead store and produce helium which then took them into the air. The reason for this evolution to take place was due to two reasons: increased competition in the water with more dangerous predators evolving and then increased population boosts in air plankton with little to eat it meant that it was more rewarding to feed in the air instead of in the water.
Behaivor
Volitare Silva unlike whales on Earth, Volitare Silva aren't intelligent creatures. In terms of behaivor, they are more comparable to an Ocean Sunfish, since all they do is float around in large groups and filter feed on the air plankton. Their main strategy is to hopefully ward off predators with their numbers and then to lay hundreds of eggs at once.
Relationships with Other Aliens
Volitare Silva's despite of how dumb they are, Volitare Silva are a keystone species due to the sheer amount of air flora and faunna that revolve around them. Similarly to Sloths, their lazy life style has made it that many species of plants and animals settle on them and rely on them for their life cycles. One of the most prominant creatures that revolve around the Volitare Silva are the Fluitantis Draconis. These small flying aliens live lives similar to eusocial insects like bees. The Queens of will make a nest on the back of the Volitare Silva, taking refuge amongnst the vegitation that forms on the back of the Volitare Silva. These small aliens in return fiercly defend the Volitare Silva from their predators, which are mainly the Spinosoa Lingua Venators.

Spinosoa Lingua Venator
Evolutionary History
The Spinosa Lingua Venators are incredibly specialized to feed specfically on the Volitare Silva. This came about due to the Volitare Silvas' population exploding intitally due to a lack of predators(since most sky predators at the time were adapted to hunt land prey and were relatively small) and a huge supply of air plankton. The Spinosoa Lingua Venators saw this gap in the food chain and soon specialized to specfically take on the Volitare Silva. These specfic adaptations to hunt the Volitare Silva are their hardened beaks(to both graple onto their round bodies and to protect their faces from the swarms of Fluitantis Draconis) and a harpoon-like tongue reinforced with bone(to penetrate their helium sacks).
Behaivor and Hunting Strategy
The Spinosoa Lingua Venator are incredibly bold in their hunting strategy, due to their size and specialized adaptations, they have nothing to fear when it comes to hunting the Volitare Silva, as the only thing they need to worry about is attacking one that has a big enough hoard of Fluitantis Draconis, as doing so could be potentially lethal. Other than, their strategy is to fly over and then attach position themselves properly using their 3 way beak before puncturing the helium sack using their tongue, then they would fly off, body in tow as they begin to liquify the insides and drink it up. Spinosoa Lingua Venators live their entire lives air born, their six wings act as oars on a giant ship, making flying a low intensity task for it. These aliens are also very terrtorial, esspecially during mating season, where males will fight using the spikes on the ends of their tails. This behaivor however is only seen in young males who are looking for a mate, older males don't engage in this behaivor as this species mates for life. Couples often are seen flying and hunting together.
