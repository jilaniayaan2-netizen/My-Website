Sol Orbis
Sol Orbis are the dominant species of plant in the nothern hemisphere of the western continent. This is due to it both its massive size(40m tall) and due to it having incredibly resiliant, thick skin. This same armored skin also covers the large sun collecting disk on the top of its body. These giants mainly live in the northen grasslands and less in the jungle, this is because the rainforests down south are too congested for these giants to grow. Sol Orbis provides a food source for the Sol Comedenti(check the Fauna Page), the largest land animals of C-100.
Cyathos Paludis
These plants fill a similar ecological niche as mangroves, acting as shelter for land animals living in the swamp from the tides and protects the smaller animals from predators. Cyathos Paludis have also evolved very dense but flexiable wood that makes them able to survive the harsh tides of the Sea of Dragons(aka The Eastern Interior Seaway). The cup-like structures that grow all over it's body are reproductive organs of the plants, similar to flowers on earth.
Victor Terram
While the Sol Comedenti(check the Fauna Page) are the largest land animals of C-100, the Victor Terram are the largest living organism. In the midwest of the western continent, they dominant an astonishing area of 5000km. While they all look like seperate organisms, they are all linked together via a network similar to mycelium networks in mushrooms and all share information and nutrients. This makes the millions, if not billions of indiviuals one super organism. They are the sole reason for the Poncu Savvannah due to preventing any other plants from growing there, effectively acting as a barrier from the rainforest.
Folium Caeli
These plants opted out of the intense competition that takes place between plants on the ground by instead evolving helium sacks to float in the air. Due to the planet's low gravity and thick atmosphere, they are able to remain suspended in the air for their entire lives, carried around by air currents. These plants act as resting places for flying creatures and as places for Volitare Silvas(Check the Fauna Page) to lay their eggs.
Colligator Aauae
Colligator Aauae are a highly specialized plants that live in the Smokey Forest biome deep in the caverns of C-100. The Smokey Forest is a biome created by a creature called Dator Vitae(Check the Fauna Page), in this biome there is no light, so how does a plant survive without light? These plants instead collect rainwater and condensed water from the cave ceiling which they then convert into sugars, similar to photosythesis but it takes out the need for sunlight entirely.
Fructus Deorum
Fructus Deorum are very typical fruit bearing plants that produce purple berries, there purple berries have a very different make up than most fruits on earth. Elira(not Miku, name change I did for a rewrite) uses these berries to make her(I accidently wrote him on the page) Purple Berry Brew which is similar to a latte or strong coffee.

Volitare Silva
Evolutionary History
Volitare Silva evolved very uniquely due to being one of the few "flying" aliens that has 4 limbs instead of the standard six limbs that most flying creatures have. These creatures' evolutionary history is very similar to that of whales on Earth. Instead of going starting out on land, Volitare Silva started out in the water. Since the atmosphere of C-100 is denser, it was much easier for them to slowly(very slowly) evolve to go into the air. Their swimbladders, which they used to control their bouyancy in the water then evolved to instead store and produce helium which then took them into the air. The reason for this evolution to take place was due to two reasons: increased competition in the water with more dangerous predators evolving and then increased population boosts in air plankton with little to eat it meant that it was more rewarding to feed in the air instead of in the water.
Behaivor
Volitare Silva unlike whales on Earth, Volitare Silva aren't intelligent creatures. In terms of behaivor, they are more comparable to an Ocean Sunfish, since all they do is float around in large groups and filter feed on the air plankton. Their main strategy is to hopefully ward off predators with their numbers and then to lay hundreds of eggs at once.
Relationships with Other Aliens
Volitare Silva's despite of how dumb they are, Volitare Silva are a keystone species due to the sheer amount of air flora and faunna that revolve around them. Similarly to Sloths, their lazy life style has made it that many species of plants and animals settle on them and rely on them for their life cycles. One of the most prominant creatures that revolve around the Volitare Silva are the Fluitantis Draconis. These small flying aliens live lives similar to eusocial insects like bees. The Queens of will make a nest on the back of the Volitare Silva, taking refuge amongnst the vegitation that forms on the back of the Volitare Silva. These small aliens in return fiercly defend the Volitare Silva from their predators, which are mainly the Spinosoa Lingua Venators.

Spinosoa Lingua Venator
Evolutionary History
The Spinosa Lingua Venators are incredibly specialized to feed specfically on the Volitare Silva. This came about due to the Volitare Silvas' population exploding intitally due to a lack of predators(since most sky predators at the time were adapted to hunt land prey and were relatively small) and a huge supply of air plankton. The Spinosoa Lingua Venators saw this gap in the food chain and soon specialized to specfically take on the Volitare Silva. These specfic adaptations to hunt the Volitare Silva are their hardened beaks(to both graple onto their round bodies and to protect their faces from the swarms of Fluitantis Draconis) and a harpoon-like tongue reinforced with bone(to penetrate their helium sacks).
Behaivor and Hunting Strategy
The Spinosoa Lingua Venator are incredibly bold in their hunting strategy, due to their size and specialized adaptations, they have nothing to fear when it comes to hunting the Volitare Silva, as the only thing they need to worry about is attacking one that has a big enough hoard of Fluitantis Draconis, as doing so could be potentially lethal. Other than, their strategy is to fly over and then attach position themselves properly using their 3 way beak before puncturing the helium sack using their tongue, then they would fly off, body in tow as they begin to liquify the insides and drink it up. Spinosoa Lingua Venators live their entire lives air born, their six wings act as oars on a giant ship, making flying a low intensity task for it. These aliens are also very terrtorial, esspecially during mating season, where males will fight using the spikes on the ends of their tails. This behaivor however is only seen in young males who are looking for a mate, older males don't engage in this behaivor as this species mates for life. Couples often are seen flying and hunting together.

Cristatus Poncu Cursor
Evolutionary History
These aliens originally lived in the rainforests of C-100 and due to this, they were much smaller as due to the sheer density of the forests, most creatures didn't have the space to get particularly large. Then, due to some kind of problem with soil(possibly a sharp increase in minerals or nitrate) caused many of the plants in the middle west of the rainforest to die. A colonial plant called "Victor Terram" had a resistance to the new toxic soil so they survived and then spread, making complex underground systems that then prevented other plants from growing in the area. This created a grasslands/savannah in the middle of the rainforest, The Cristatus Poncu Cursor took advantage of the new open space and in a few generations got much larger from their original sized(around a tapier) to large herbivores. Other than size, nothing else significantly changed fom when they were smaller. These creatures evolved to have 5 pairs of eyes early on as they were commonly predated on and it gave them near 360 degree vision. Cristatus Poncu Cursor kept this adaptation(unlike their cousins Cristatus Serpens which only kept 4 pairs of eyes instead of 5). The reason for keeping this adaptation is because of the giant predators that came out of the formation of the Savvannah(in particular Armato Rege and Terram Cete) so having the extra vision allowed them to warn their herds better. This species also displays sexual dimorphism, with the males having giant colorful frills which they use to compete for mates and heirarchy in the herd.
Behaivor
Cristatus Poncu Cursor herds have 1 "alpha" which is a male that ensures the safety of the rest of the herd, decides where they graze, really any big decision for the herd would be made by this indivual. Herds were made up of both males and females, the prescense of other males obviously leads to conflict, esspecially during mating season, but sometimes other males will challenge the alpha in an attempt to take control of the herd. Now, some males will also decide to leave the herd entirely and join a new herd but that is rare. Now, how are these disputes between males settled? They aren't settled with wrestling matches or by fighting, instead the males will circle one another whilst showing off their giant frills and singing(their singing sounds similar to a sharp whistling). Whoever has the bigger, more colorful frill and the better singing wins the dispute.

Armato Rege
Evolutionary History
Armato Rege is by all definitions a super predator. It is one of the very few hexapodal vertebrates that lives on land instead of in the air and that gives it unique advantages compared to most of the 4 limbed aliens on the ground. Other features that make Armato Rege an apex predator are; keratinous armor, double jointed front limbs, and large size. Now how did something like this evolve? Originally, Armato Rege lived only in the northern regions, due to the grasslands, abundant large prey, and lack of competition. The Armato Rege also began migrating southward as the population increased, this is due to younger indivuals trying to find their own territories. As for how they evolved, they first started off being medium sized predators, with other larger and more simple predators being apexes at the time. As the herbivores evolved more defensive traits, these predators couldn't keep up and instead the armored and more specialized Armato Rege had more success. This success led them to evolve to be larger. Another evolutionary trait that allowed them to outcompete the older, more simple aliens was it's two front limbs. The two front limbs are double jointed, which allows it to use these limbs as both legs and arms. Meaning, they can be used to slam, pin, throw, and position prey. Another interesting thing to point out about Armato Rege is that there are differences in populations in the north and the middle west. In the north, the armor isn't as dense(and they are smaller), making the northern population faster but less tanky, where as the middle west population has denser armor which makes it slower but tankier. Now, it isn't a huge difference but it is significant enough to notice. The reason for this is because in the middle west there are larger predators that at one point were able to hunt the less armored and smaller Armato Reges(The main alien that could do this was Terram Cete.), so they were forced to evolve thicker armor and larger size. Armato Rege also evolved to have some intelligence, similar to how T-Rex did, the reason for this was for 3 main reasons: raising young, locating large prey, and succesfully strategizing to take down prey.
Behaivor and Hunting Strategy
Armato Rege's hunting strategy is quite simple, they use their coloration to blend in with the tree line before ambushing prey. Adult Armato Rege are mostly solitary creatures that control vast swaths of territory, the only times that full grown adults interact with other adults is either: Disputes over territory or mates and mating. Females ussually have 1 offspring which they usually raise on their own but there are chances where 2 offspring are born from 1 mating, which in that case, 1 offspring is raised by the female and the other by the male. The young stay with their parent for 2 years before going out on their own. Juviniles at this point are still somewhat vunerable so they will from groups of 3-5 which are called "Terrors". Terrors are called that because they are just that, its like a group of teenagers except they are all already 6'3. In the middle west, Terrors will sometimes try hunting Terram Cete. They hunt these giants by first sending one out to the burrow of the Terram Cete as bait, when it pops out, the others will then pin it down while the one used as bait finishes it off by going for soft tissue around the neck. This can obviously go incredibly wrong and Terrors will only attempt this high risk, high reward hunt when they are desperate. Terrors can also be threatened by adult Armato Reges. Terrors ussually only last for about 3 years because by then, they are ready to find their own territories.

Poncu Foidens
Evolutionary History
Believe it or not, Poncu Foidens is distantly related to both Cristatus Serpens and Cristatus Poncu Cursor. This is obviously surprising because they are much smaller than both and they only have two large pairs of eyes instead of the 4 or 5 pairs that their relatives have. Now they are distantly related, meaning that they had a common ancestor very very long ago, meanwhile their two relatives are closer with one another. The reason they evolved 1 pair of eyes is because they are nocternal, having two large eyes is better than have 8 or 10 small eyes since two large eyes captured more light in low light conditions. They fill a niche similar to rabbits, small herbivores(mostly) that live mostly in burrows. The antennae are used for non verbal communication, by moving the atennae to for different shapes they can communicate different things, similar to sign language. Like their relatives, this species display sexual dimorphism, with males having small frills.

Volantem Interfectorem
Evolutionary History
This creature, similarly to Spinosoa Lingua Venator has six limbs, two legs and 4 wings. It is a medium sized predator that has specialized in dive bombing tactics to catch prey, similar to falcons on Earth. They evolved to have flat, wedge-like bodies that make them very areodynamic which lets them accelerate to incredibly high speeds. The two things that sets this alien apart from many of the other medium sized ariel predators are it's sideways jaws and complex eyes. The sideways jaws were adapted to make it able to swoop right above the ground and snap up prey with ease that normally oriented jaws wouldn't allow. The complex eyes are for spotting prey amongnst the dense vegitation of the rainforests and along the tall grasslands of the western continent.
Behaivor and Hunting Strategy/header>
This alien hunts very similarly to a giant falcon, they climb up high before tucking their wings and diving. The difference however, is that they won't dive right above their prey, instead they will dive some distance away before flying low above the ground(incredibly fast due to the momentum from the dive) and then catch their prey with their sideways jaws. These aliens will migrate to the mountains during their mating period to find mates and build nests. These aliens are also very curious and smart, some being seen even using tools(very rudementary). The tool use is ussually dropping rocks on the ground to drive out prey.
